## Before the tutorial

Bring last week's save, with roboport coverage and bots already available.
The [factory simulator](/simulator/) can check that a candidate layout tiles
cleanly before you spend bot time capturing the blueprint.

## In the tutorial

Design your production block, capture it as a blueprint, and place a
second copy directly adjacent to the first to confirm the edges line up.

## Afterwards

Keep this blueprint — Week 11's megabase-scale work assumes tiling
discipline like this is already solved.
