## Before the tutorial

Come with a preference for which subsystem you'd want to own — smelting,
science, defence or rail — but be ready to negotiate.

## In the tutorial

Form your group, allocate roles, and agree on the shared conventions your
project save will use before anyone places a single belt on it.

## Afterwards

The shared save you start today is the one Assignment 2 marks at the end
of Week 12.
