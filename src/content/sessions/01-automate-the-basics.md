## Before the tutorial

Read this week's lecture. Bring a fresh save — this practical is only
meaningful if it starts from nothing.

## In the tutorial

Mine the starting patches by hand, set up a manual smelting line sized to
what your mining rate can actually feed, and get enough power running to
research Automation. Nothing here should involve a belt or an assembler
you didn't already have before Automation unlocked it.

## Afterwards

Bring your save to the next tutorial — Week 2 builds directly on the base
you leave this one with.
