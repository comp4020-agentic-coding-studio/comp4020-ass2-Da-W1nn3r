## Before the tutorial

This is the last tutorial of semester — bring every save you've built
across the course if you want to compare your own base's TPS along the
way.

## In the tutorial

Audit the supplied base against this week's checklist: entity count,
entity type, pollution and biter activity. Say what's already been
optimised and what, if anything, is left.
