## Before the tutorial

Bring last week's save. Both comparison bases are supplied at the start of
the tutorial.

## In the tutorial

Run both supplied bases side by side, measure their actual throughput, and
try to trace a deliberately introduced fault in each. Write up which
design coped better and why.

## Afterwards

Keep your written comparison — it's referenced again when Assignment 1
asks you to diagnose a base under the same lens.
