## Before the tutorial

Bring last week's save, with a bus or equivalent supply of iron and copper
plate already running.

## In the tutorial

Work out the ratio of assemblers needed at each step of your chosen
science pack's chain, build it, and run it long enough to confirm nothing
starves or backs up.

## Afterwards

This chain is exactly what Week 8's diagnostic tutorial expects you to be
able to check when something in it stops matching its ratios.
