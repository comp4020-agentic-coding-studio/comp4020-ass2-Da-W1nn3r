## Before the tutorial

Bring last week's save. Check your base's current pollution cloud before
you start — it affects what you're walking into.

## In the tutorial

Plan an approach to the assigned nest using this week's tools, clear it,
and use the terrain change to open a route that was previously blocked.

## Afterwards

Whatever route or terrain change you make here, Week 6's rail line has to
work around or through it.
