## Before the tutorial

Bring last week's save. Both comparison designs are supplied at the start
of the tutorial.

## In the tutorial

Examine both supplied bases, work out what each one is actually
optimising for, and explain how specific choices in each layout serve
that target.

## Afterwards

The distinction between optimising for output and optimising for speed
carries directly into Week 12's TPS work.
