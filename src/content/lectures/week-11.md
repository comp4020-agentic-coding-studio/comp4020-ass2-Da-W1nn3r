## Outline

- **Megabases and travel time** — once a base is minutes to walk across,
  travel time itself becomes a design cost.
- **TPS basics** — the game's simulation tick rate as a real, finite
  resource: every entity updated each tick is a small cost.
- **Expansion** — growing outward with new outposts versus growing upward
  in productivity per tile.
- **Speedrunning** — minimising time to a fixed goal rather than
  maximising steady-state output, which changes which techniques matter.
- **Designing blueprints for speed** — how a blueprint optimised for
  minimum build time differs from one optimised for long-term throughput.
