## Outline

- **Fluids on trains** — fluid wagons as the long-distance answer to pipe
  throughput limits.
- **Trains** — locomotive and wagon fundamentals: acceleration, braking
  distance, and train length as a real design constraint.
- **Intersections** — signal patterns that keep crossing lines independent
  versus ones that quietly create a standoff under load.
- **Stations** — loading and unloading design, train stop naming, and why
  station throughput is usually the actual bottleneck.
- **Automating train lines** — automatic mode, named stops and simple
  conditions running a schedule with no manual driving.
