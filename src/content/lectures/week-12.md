## Outline

- **TPS advanced** — entity count and entity *type*, since inserters,
  loaded belts and biters cost more per tick than others.
- **Pollution TPS** — pollution cloud simulation and biter activity as a
  TPS cost separate from production.
- **Reducing base footprint** — revisiting Week 2's footprint trade-off
  with a TPS lens.
- **What causes lower TPS** — a checklist: entity count, entity type,
  pollution and biter activity, and how to tell which is responsible for a
  given slowdown.
