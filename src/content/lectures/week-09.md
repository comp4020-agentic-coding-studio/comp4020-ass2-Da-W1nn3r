## Outline

- **Bots** — construction robots as the mechanism that turns a blueprint
  into a built structure, and the roboport coverage that determines how
  fast a large blueprint goes up.
- **Blueprints** — capturing a working design, wiring and signal
  conditions intact, so it can be placed again exactly.
- **Tiling** — designing a blueprint's edges deliberately so copies placed
  adjacent connect without manual patching.
