## Outline

- **Brownouts** — what happens when power demand outstrips supply, and why
  a brownout is often the first visible symptom of a problem that started
  elsewhere.
- **Solar and accumulator ratio** — sizing a solar field against
  accumulator storage so the base survives the night.
- **Identifying bottlenecks** — a method, not a guess: checking machine
  utilisation, buffer levels and belt saturation in order, from the symptom
  backwards to the constrained step.
- **Parallel versus series production** — adding another identical line
  against speeding up one, and why they aren't interchangeable once ratios
  are already tight.
- **Discussion: the everlasting chip shortage** — circuits as the base's
  perennial bottleneck, and why "build more green circuits" is rarely the
  whole fix.
