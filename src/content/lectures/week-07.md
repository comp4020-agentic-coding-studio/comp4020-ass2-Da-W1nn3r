## Outline

- **Multiplayer and the inefficiency of differing solutions** — what goes
  wrong when several people design the same subsystem independently: this
  week is the pivot from individual technique to team process.
- **Role allocation** — splitting a shared base into owned zones or
  subsystems so decisions have one clear owner.
- **Management** — lightweight coordination habits that scale to a small
  team: shared belt colour conventions, train stop naming, and agreeing on
  bus contents before anyone starts building.
