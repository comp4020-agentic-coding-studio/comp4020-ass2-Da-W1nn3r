## Outline

- **Splitters** — merging and dividing belt lines, and the default
  equal-split behaviour that never crosses a belt's two lanes.
- **T-junctions** — belts merging into the side of another belt instead of
  through a splitter, and how that collapses both lanes of the merging belt
  onto a single lane of the one it joins.
- **Inserters** — reach, rotation, stack size, and filtering as a control
  element, not just a mover.
- **Underground belts** — crossing belts and pipes without a physical
  intersection, and their maximum gap length.
- **Belt sides** — left-lane versus right-lane loading, and the real cost
  of splitting two items back apart cleanly.
- **Buffers** — chests or belt segments that absorb short-term demand
  spikes.
- **Balancers** — built from splitters and T-junctions together, and the
  difference between a design that only balances belts and one that
  balances belts and lanes both, fully throughput-unlimited.
