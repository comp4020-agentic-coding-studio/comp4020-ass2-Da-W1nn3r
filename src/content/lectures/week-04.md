## Outline

- **Input/output ratios** — reading a recipe's crafting time and
  ingredient count as a rate problem.
- **Fluid systems** — why fluids don't have discrete belt "slots," and the
  basic refinery-to-chemical-plant chain.
- **Crafting time and ratios** — why a ratio that balances two machines
  doesn't automatically balance a five-step chain.
- **Modules and beacons** — speed, productivity and efficiency modules as a
  ratio decision, not just a power-cost one.
- **On-site versus belted-in intermediates** — local complexity against bus
  congestion.
- **Chaining assembling machines** — a worked example showing how ratio
  mismatches compound down a chain.
