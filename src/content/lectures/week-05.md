## Outline

- **Transport options** — car, tank, train and spidertron against the
  baseline of walking, each with its own speed and cargo trade-offs.
- **Obstacle removal** — cliff explosives and landfill as deliberate
  terrain costs.
- **Biters, worms and nests** — how enemy bases grow with pollution and
  evolution, and why walling off only postpones the problem.
- **Turret creep, firepower and kiting** — advancing a turret line safely,
  and using speed to kite biters away from a weak point.
- **Other tools** — poison capsules and combat robots for situations a
  straight firepower fight doesn't suit.
- **Walls** — where a wall earns its cost, as a chokepoint for turrets
  rather than a substitute for defence.
