## Outline

- **Logic gates** — comparators and decider/arithmetic combinators as the
  circuit-network equivalent of an if-statement.
- **Nuclear setups** — reactor and heat-exchanger layout, why neighbouring
  reactors boost each other, and the failure mode logic gates exist to
  prevent.
- **Logic-based rails** — circuit conditions on train stops and signals so
  trains run only when the network's state calls for it.
- **On-demand trains** — dispatching a train only when a station's stock
  actually needs topping up.
