## The brief

> Given diagrams and short scenarios, not a running game, solve each on
> paper.

There is no Factorio client in the exam room. Every puzzle — a balancer
that needs proving fair, a rail intersection that needs a deadlock found in
it, a production line with an unlabelled bottleneck, a base described just
well enough to say why its TPS is low — is answered from the diagrams and
numbers given. The exam tests whether the semester's habits survive without
the game in front of you to check them against.

## What you submit

Written answers to each puzzle in the set, submitted in the exam room under
standard invigilated conditions.
