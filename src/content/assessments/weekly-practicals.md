## The brief

> Complete and submit each tutorial's practical during, or shortly after,
> that week.

Every tutorial page states a practical and a goal. This assessment is the
running tally of those twelve submissions across the semester — the habit of
finishing the week's task, not a single deliverable of its own.

## What you submit

Whatever each tutorial's practical calls for, submitted through that week's
tutorial page. This assessment's mark is the completeness and quality of the
set, assessed once at the end of semester.
