## The brief

> As a group, take a shared base from where Week 7 leaves it to a
> self-sufficient Space Science operation.

Groups form in Week 7 and inherit one save between them. Everything from
that point — role allocation, which sub-system each member owns, how
conflicting blueprint choices get resolved — is part of the assessment, not
incidental to it. A base that works because one person did everything is not
a passing group project, however good the base is.

## What you submit

The shared save file at hand-in, a design report covering the production
chain's key ratios and the layout decisions that made them hold, and a short
note from each member on what they built and what they relied on a teammate
for.
